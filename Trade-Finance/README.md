# 💱 Trade Finance Platform

## 📌 Overview

**Trade Finance** is a conceptual and technical project that demonstrates the **end-to-end lifecycle of trade finance operations** used in global commerce.  
The project focuses on digitizing, automating, and managing trade finance instruments such as **Letters of Credit (LC)**, **Bank Guarantees**, **Bills of Lading**, **Invoices**, and **Payments**.

It is designed for **banks, financial institutions, fintechs, and developers** exploring modernization of trade finance using technology.

---

## 🏦 What is Trade Finance?

Trade Finance enables importers and exporters to conduct international trade by mitigating risks related to:
- Payment delays
- Counterparty trust
- Currency and compliance issues
- Cross-border regulations

### Key Instruments
- Letter of Credit (LC)
- Standby LC
- Bank Guarantees
- Documentary Collections
- Invoice Financing
- Supply Chain Finance

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Digitize traditional trade finance workflows
- Improve transparency and traceability
- Reduce manual paperwork and processing time
- Enable audit-ready and compliant transactions
- Serve as a **PoC / reference architecture** for trade finance systems
